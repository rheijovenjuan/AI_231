What Whisper does

Audio → text caption (that's it)

  • faster-whisper base.en (int8, ~74 M params) — encoder-decoder transformer, runs on-device, no cloud.
  • After the wake word it writes the sentence: "set alarm for 8 am" — a subtitle, not a decision.
  • ~1.1 s per clip (slowest step) — excluded from every benchmark number.
  • Not an LLM: no knowledge, no reasoning — it copies what it heard.

Where the caption is used

  • Display — show "you said: …" in the log/GUI.
  • Free-form numbers — slot policy text: "dim to 35 percent" (values outside the fixed 19) can override the keyword's value.
  • Second opinion on intent — see the voting rules below.

In plain terms

  • Whisper is a tie-breaker / helper, never a dictator: a wrong caption cannot overrule a confident keyword hit.
  • Slots: with default slot_policy=text the caption's number may override the keyword's value — keywords still decide which slot.
  • python app.py --mode mic --no-asr → Whisper never loads: intent + slots decided 100 % by the VCM (the configuration for requirements #7/#8).
